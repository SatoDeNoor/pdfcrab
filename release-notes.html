What's New in v1.4

Released 2026

Fill & export forms

Fill interactive PDF form fields directly in the app, then export the completed form.

Search in your PDF

What's New in v1.3.1

Released 2026

Mac App Store

PDFCrab is now available on Mac. All features from the iOS app run natively on macOS — edit, OCR, export, manage pages, and password-protect PDFs directly on your Mac. No cloud, no subscriptions, nothing leaves your device.

Arabic language support

Text editing and OCR now correctly recognize and render Arabic script with right-to-left layout. The right font is embedded automatically based on what the text contains.

What's New in v1.3

Released 2026

OCR

Scan a photo or PDF of a document and PDFCrab recognizes the text on-device, turning it into real, selectable, editable PDF text instead of a flat image. Review and correct recognized text, then save as separate files or merged into one PDF.

Cyrillic, Chinese, Japanese & Korean support

Editing text and recognizing text via OCR now correctly handles Cyrillic and CJK scripts, not just Latin.

Export to 5 formats

Turn any PDF into a TXT file, an audio narration, page images, a Word document, or a PowerPoint — directly on your device, for every PDF, not just scanned ones.

Manage Pages

The screen you already use to delete pages also lets you drag pages into a new order and save when you're ready.

Page Tools

Add page numbers in the position you choose, rotate any page, and switch between Standard and Letter page sizes — with a one-tap way back to the original size.

More fonts

Choose from 9 new fonts when editing or adding text — Inter, Roboto, Lato, Merriweather, Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Open Sans, Source Sans 3, and JetBrains Mono. Preview the font and size live before applying.

Page navigation sidebar

Jump to any page instantly with the new slide-out page sidebar.
