Privacy

PDFCrab processes all files locally on your device. No files, text, or personal data are uploaded to any server or third party.

FAQ

Can I edit password-protected PDFs?
Yes. You will be prompted to enter the password when opening the file. The file remains encrypted after editing.

What PDF features are supported?
Text editing and repositioning, image editing, and vector object editing on most standard PDFs.
