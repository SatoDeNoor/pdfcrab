Privacy

PDFCrab processes all files locally on your device. No files, text, or personal data are uploaded to any server or third party.

FAQ

Can I edit password-protected PDFs?
Yes. You will be prompted to enter the password when opening the file. The file stays encrypted after editing. You can also add, remove, or change the password from the PDF Password screen.

How do I merge PDFs?
Tap Merge PDFs on the home screen, pick two or more files, reorder pages if needed, then tap Merge. The result is saved as a new file.

How do I delete pages?
Tap Delete Pages on the home screen, select the pages you want to remove from the thumbnail grid, then tap Delete.

What PDF features are supported?
Text editing and repositioning, image editing (move, resize, delete), vector object editing, page deletion, PDF merging, and password management.

Does PDFCrab upload my files?
No. All processing happens on-device. Your files never leave your iPhone.
