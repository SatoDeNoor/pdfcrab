Privacy Policy

PDFCrab

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Overview

PDFCrab is a PDF editing application for iOS. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

PDFCrab does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that access your data.

PDF Processing

All PDF processing happens entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server and no file content leaves your device.

File Access

The app accesses only the PDF files you explicitly open via the system file picker. Files are not copied, backed up, or shared by the app.
