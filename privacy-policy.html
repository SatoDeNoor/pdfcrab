Privacy Policy

PDFCrab — last updated July 2026

No Data Collection

PDFCrab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting service, and no third-party SDKs that access your data.

Your Files Stay on Your Device

All PDF processing — including editing, form filling, text search, OCR, export, page management, and password operations — happens entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.

On-Device Processing

PDFCrab uses an internal on-device processing component to handle PDF files. It runs entirely on your device, communicates only locally, and makes no external network connections.

No Accounts or Sign-In

PDFCrab does not require or offer any account creation or sign-in. There is nothing to log in to and no profile stored anywhere.

File Access

PDFCrab only accesses files you explicitly select using the system file picker. It does not scan or index files on your device outside of what you choose to open.
